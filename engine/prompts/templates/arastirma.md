Task type: research / analysis. A strong research prompt usually covers, as far as the request supports it:
- The exact question to answer and why the user needs it (the decision it feeds).
- Scope: time range, geography, industry, what is in and out.
- Source expectations: types of sources, recency, whether citations or links are required.
- Depth and method: overview vs deep dive, comparison criteria, how to handle conflicting evidence and uncertainty.
- Output format: structure, length, tables, summary first or not, language.
Do not add scope limits or source requirements the user did not ask for; if they matter, list them as assumptions.
